PDF Kite Support

For help with PDF Kite, email varindersingh24230@gmail.com. Please include your phone model and the tool you were using.

Where are my files?

Every file you create is listed under My files: tap the folder icon at the top of the home screen. Use Save or Share on the result screen to keep a copy somewhere else.

Do my documents get uploaded?

No. Every tool runs on your phone. Translation downloads a language pack the first time you use a language, and Web page to PDF opens the address you enter.

Why am I asked to watch an ad?

PDF Kite has no paid plan. OCR, Translate and the Word, Excel and PowerPoint exports ask for one short video, which unlocks them for 30 minutes. If no ad is available, the tool runs anyway.
